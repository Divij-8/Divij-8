## About

I'm a **B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at KL University Hyderabad**, focused on understanding how real software systems are designed, secured, and operated.

My interests sit between **backend engineering and AI systems** — building APIs and data-intensive services, designing reliable state transitions, working with AI-agent infrastructure, and exploring how security and distributed systems shape modern applications.

I prefer projects where the interesting part is not just the interface, but the **architecture underneath it**: consistency, transactions, authentication, observability, execution boundaries, and the systems that make software dependable.

> **I build to understand systems — then rebuild them better.**
