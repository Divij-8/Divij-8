## ABOUT

I'm a B.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at KL University Hyderabad, focused on how real software systems are designed, secured and operated.

My work sits between backend engineering and AI systems: APIs and data-intensive services, reliable state transitions, and the infrastructure that lets AI agents call tools safely. I'm most interested in the layer underneath the interface: consistency, transactions, authentication, observability and execution boundaries.

I build to understand a system, then rebuild it better.
